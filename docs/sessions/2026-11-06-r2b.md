## Decision before code

[R2B_SPEC.md](../R2B_SPEC.md) and D37 were written before model code:

- levels 0-4;
- per-lot capacities 0/4/8/20/40 residents, 0/2/4/10/20 commercial jobs and 0/4/8/16/28 industrial jobs (levels 1-2 unchanged);
- land-value gates for levels 3 and 4, with drops above the cap;
- leaving takes the highest levels first;
- a display-only wealth band;
- new stage art;
- schema 10 / `r2b-1`.

**Gate revisions during implementation, before delivery.** The first draft had commercial gates equal to residential and industry ungated. The source tests showed that this changed the standard town: trade (+2) and ungated industry concentrated levels on single lots. That contradicted the goal that plain streets stop at level 2. The adopted rules are:

- commercial lots need 2 more land value (3 / 4: trade plus a park);
- industry needs a workforce of 100 / 250 town residents (prior state).

Both are recorded in the specification and D37. With them, the standard town and every earlier expectation are unchanged.
