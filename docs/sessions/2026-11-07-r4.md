## Decision before code

[R4_SPEC.md](../R4_SPEC.md) and D40 were written and adopted before code:

- five short sounds and one 12-second music loop, **synthesised at run time by our own code** (no audio files, samples, packs, downloads or generation services);
- Master / Sounds / Music buses with defaults 80 / 70 / 30%, Mute all and M, in a Sound & motion dialog;
- calm water, smoke and flag animation from real time, with an off switch;
- settings version 2, migrating version 1.

**Revision during implementation, before delivery.** The first native run showed the Display dialog running off the bottom of the 720p window once the Sound & motion button was added (the headless check had passed). Center view and Sound & motion now share a row, and the packed check measures the dialog's position as well as its size. A second native run confirmed the fix.

## Implementation

- **New `presentation/sound_kit.gd`:** tones (sine, triangle) with attack, exponential decay and an end fade; our own linear-congruential noise with a low-pass; five effect recipes; a pad of four chords plus a 16-note melody at 80 BPM; peak measurement and conversion to 16-bit mono `AudioStreamWAV` (looping for music).
- **New `presentation/audio_director.gd`:** creates the Effects and Music buses, the effect streams, four effect players and the music player; makes the music on a background thread; applies volumes (0% mutes a bus) and Mute all; plays effects and records the last one.
- **`main.gd`:** build / clear / reject sounds from commands; a grow chime once per frame that raised total levels; an alert when a settlement closes with a deficit; the real-time animation clock (about ten redraws a second); the Sound & motion dialog and the Display row; M; settings values and their application; Help.
- **`world_view.gd`:** water shimmer (4 s), industry smoke (3 s) in every art mode and the breathing link flag (2.4 s); `anim_time` 0 is the static frame.
- **`settings_store.gd`:** version 2 with volume and flag validation, and version-1 migration.
- `docs/BUILD_README.txt`, `licenses/ASSETS.md` (an audio ledger), version 0.17.0-r4. No save change: schema 12 / `r3-1`.
