## Implemented behaviour

**Desirability (derived, never saved).**

- **Score.** Each home lot scores:
  - +2 for a park within 3 side-steps (the D26 coverage, not stacking);
  - +1 for a water tile within 3;
  - −2 for each developed workshop lot within 2 side-steps, counting at most three.
  - Distances are footprint to footprint, so a workshop across a one-tile road counts.
- **Rebuilds.** The score is rebuilt with growth totals after every command, growth round, tax change and load. The water field is recomputed with the other derived state.
- **Overlay field.** A per-tile field uses the same parts. A home lot's tiles show that lot's score.

**Effects on homes.**

- **Order.** Home demand is reserved by score (higher first), then map order. This generalises D26's park-first order. Shops and workshops keep map order.
- **Held and dropping.** A home scoring below 0 develops only to level 1. A serviced level-2 home whose score turns negative drops one level at the next round.
- **With tax leaving.** Drops count towards D32 leaving for that round, so no lot ever loses more than one level per round.
- **Recovery and safety.** Recovery is ordinary growth once the score is 0 or more, for example after adding a park or clearing the workshop. Nothing is demolished, and cash, zones and the project are untouched.

**Interface.**

- **V and Display.** **V** and a Display button toggle the overlay. It is off at start and never saved.
  - Positive tiles get a light tint and one small "+" per point.
  - Negative tiles get a warm tint and diagonal hatching: one stripe per 2 points, rounded up, at most 3, thickened after the first native look.
  - Developed workshops show a smoke mark.
- **Inspector.**
  - **Legend line.** For a home lot it reads "Desirability N: park +2, water +1, workshops −2", listing only non-zero parts. Otherwise it shows the marks legend, or the overlay legend while the overlay is on.
  - **New reasons.** "Held at L1 by workshops: add a park or move them" and "Drops to L1 next check (workshops): add a park".
  - **Fit.** The inspector widens at larger text (480 / 520 px at text 18 / 20) so the longest texts fit its fixed height.
- **Other wording.**
  - Help gains three lines.
  - The park tooltip and inspector now describe the +2.
  - The demand prefix under high taxes is shortened to "(taxes)".
  - A format-7 load says "every lot kept" and keeps that save's own tax rate.

**Saves.**

- **Format.** Schema 8 / `m2g-1` has the same fields as schema 7, because desirability is derived.
- **Migration.** Schema 7 migrates with every value kept, and schemas 1-6 as before.
- **Preservation.** Older primary and backup bytes are preserved as `manual.schemaN.json`, now including schema 7.
- **Fixtures.** The eight earlier fixtures are byte-identical, and `m2g-schema8.json` is added.

## Effect on existing expectations

**Standard test town.** The shared test town has six home lots across a street from three shop and three workshop lots. It now settles at **40 residents / 18 jobs**, not 48 / 18.

- The two homes opposite the developed workshops are held at level 1.
- The third workshop never develops, so the home opposite it is not held.
- Two home levels of demand stay unusable.

**Updated expectations.** Growth, economy, tax and packed expectations that depended on 48 / 18 were updated by reasoning from the rules, then confirmed with a probe (`builds/validation/m2g_probe.gd`, ignored).

**Examples.**

- The second-month settlement is $172 net instead of $188.
- The 0% demand is 7 / 1 / 1 instead of 5 / 1 / 1.
- The 25% surplus is 5 / 2 / 2 instead of 7 / 2 / 2.
- The leaving order follows the new level distribution.

The shipped-layout packed flow settles the same way.

## Limits

- **Native coverage.** Native evidence is 1280x720 at standard text on one machine. The inspector at larger text was checked by automated layout only.
- **Tuning.** The radii and values are provisional and untuned by human play.
- **Overlay legibility.** At 1x zoom the overlay is legible but subtle.
- **Not sources yet.** Stations and roads cause no nuisance, and shops and workshops have no desirability.
- **Unverified.** Human usability and enjoyment, DPI variants, other hardware and operating systems, the controller, performance and CI.
- **Not covered by this work.** The five-person M3 playtest gate.
