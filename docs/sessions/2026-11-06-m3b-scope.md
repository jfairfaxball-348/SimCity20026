## Owner decisions during the session

While the guide was being specified, the owner made two decisions:

- **U16:** homes, shops and workshops are 4x4-tile buildings, and roads stay one tile.
- **U17:** lean closer to the classic city-builder design, overruling earlier design-scope limits, provided no copyright rules are broken.

In response:

- **M2e lots (proposed D30).** [M2e](../M2E_SPEC.md) specifies freely placed 4x4 lots that replace plots one-for-one, keeping D19-D28 numbers. It uses schema 6 / `m2e-1`, and older single-tile zones are cleared on migration. M2e becomes the next implementation session.
- **Genre candidates.** [Genre-alignment candidates](../GENRE_ALIGNMENT.md) lists nine classic-genre systems for the owner to choose from; none is adopted.
- **Guide order.** The M3b guide gained a dependency section and now follows M2e.
- **Recorded boundary.** The rights boundary, the U10 clean-room rule and the rule against recreating the reference game's look or identity are unchanged. AGENTS.md and IP_AND_ASSETS.md record the owner's direction alongside them.

The handoff was updated: the next session implements M2e only.

No game code, assets, dependencies, fixtures, user saves or binary outputs changed. No reference source was opened or run, and no external research, purchases, messages, public release or licence changes took place.
