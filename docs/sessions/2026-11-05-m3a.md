5 November 2026. **0.6.0-m3a / schema 5 / m3a-1, implemented and tested locally.** The owner requested the next session and continued it. Scope is the single project specified in M3A_SPEC.md; full M3 and public release remain future work.

## Baseline and implementation

Clean local/default and live remote HEAD agreed at `4631b3e99dfa822f9773af33057af9b0a3003fb1`. Created `codex/m3a-project`; adopted D28 before model edits. Reviewed current instructions, handoff/specification, Decisions, toolchain, M2d record and original model/UI/storage/tests. No reference source was opened or run; no dependencies, external assets, purchases, external messages or licence changes.

One optional project, A welcoming square: explicitly complete a chosen park with three distinct developed HOME plots, each serviced by road and power and within radius 3. Completion is free, one-time, ordered, and changes only completion/anchor plus command sequence. Flower geometry is original and cosmetic. Decline cannot erase completion; clearing its park removes the anchor and flowers, retaining history. Prices, allocation, growth, park priority, accounting and clock order remain unchanged.

The J/Project modal lists parks in row-major order, explains count/requirements and cosmetic result, and offers Complete square / Show park. Show park closes and centres the view; a completed park has a written inspector explanation. Help is scrollable. The button shares the existing funds area with Budget, after packed tests caught sidebar crowding at 720p. No broad UI redesign or onboarding was added.

Schema 5 adds a strict completed Boolean and park index (-1 when absent). Schema-4 budget validation now remains explicitly historical rather than depending on the current schema constant. Schemas 1-4 gain an incomplete project without automatic completion or retrospective accounting. Existing generic store preservation handles schema-4 originals; failure injection verifies primary and backup cases. No store rewrite was needed. Five historical fixtures remain byte-identical; a sixth captures a completed square at tick 59 with fractional time and prior ledger.
