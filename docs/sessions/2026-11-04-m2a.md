## Implemented contract

Decisions D17–D18 were recorded before coding. The outside link is a persistent visual marker on boundary land, normally the east-edge midpoint `(63,32)`. Custom saved terrain uses the nearest boundary land by Manhattan distance and row-major tie-break; an all-water boundary has no link and a clear explanation. Root selection uses no RNG or occupancy state.

An ordinary paid road on the marker activates the link. An iterative, fixed-order orthogonal flood fill marks reachable roads and side-adjacent home plots. Other edges, diagonals, homes, and empty ground do not transmit access. Removing a bridge/root immediately removes service; reconnection restores it. Failed commands and previews change nothing.

`sim/road_access.gd` is scene-independent. Initialization, validated decoding, and every successful occupancy-changing command rebuild its two derived arrays. Home edits also update service. Ticks/frames do not rebuild topology. No new serialized field or ID was added. Schema **1**, simulation/content **m1-1**, original generation, RNG, clock, and prices remain unchanged; this slice adds no economic/growth effect.

The UI adds an original gold flag, a named outside-link control/Home shortcut, hover explanations, and optional R plus/minus access marks. The inspector distinguishes no neighbouring road, an isolated road district, connected access, inactive/blocked link, and unavailable access. It always states that growth/power are unimplemented. The flag is contained inside its tile and does not cover neighbouring roads. Small loaded maps now center safely within the camera bounds.

## Corrections found during verification

- Initial native autowrap requested an excessively tall inspector. Establishing label widths before the first layout fixed this; first-frame geometry now has explicit checks.
- The first headless startup assertion used the runner's default 64×64 viewport. The packed driver now sets the real 1280×720 startup size before instantiating the scene.
- Large-text explanations needed a taller inspector, and placement errors could enlarge the sidebar into the footer. The inspector now has bounded width/height choices, the camera leaves the link above it, and compact controls reserve room for rejected-stroke text.
- A wide label over the outside flag obscured nearby roads. A compact original flag stays within the root tile; the inspector/button provide its readable name.
- The pre-existing camera bounds inverted on maps narrower than the viewport. Small maps are now centered, with regression checks at all three zoom levels.
