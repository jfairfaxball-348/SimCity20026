The scope is the [M2f specification](../M2F_SPEC.md): candidates 9 and 4 of the [U24 city-builder roadmap](../GENRE_ALIGNMENT.md). ## Implemented behaviour

**Tax rate.**

- The town stores a whole-percent `tax_rate` from 0 to 25, standard 10.
- Each settlement charges 20 cents per point per serviced resident and 40 cents per point per serviced job, at the rate in force then. The standard rate is therefore exactly D25.
- `set_tax_rate` is an ordered, validated, free command.
  - The current rate is a no-op without a sequence step.
  - It rejects non-integers and out-of-range values, and is refused at the command limit.
  - It refreshes demand and the forecast immediately, even while paused, and consumes no RNG.

**Demand under taxes.**

- Appeal = 100 + 4 × (10 − rate) percent. It multiplies the D22 targets, rounding half up in integers.
- Targets are unchanged at 10%. The empty-town starter is 1 home level at 25% and 4 at 0%.

**Leaving.**

- **When.** Only above 10%. Each type's surplus (levels above target) loses at most 4 levels per growth round.
- **Which lots.** One level from each chosen developed, serviced lot: level 2 first, then later map order. Unserviced lots are left to service-loss decline.
- **What stays.** Zones, cash and the project are never removed.
- **Settled sizes.** In the standard lot town the equilibrium moves from 48 residents / 18 jobs at 10% to 28 / 12 at 15%. Lowering the rate lets the town regrow.

**Interface.**

- **Meter.** A compact H/S/W meter sits under the population count. Each entry has its letter, a small bar from a centre line (up for demand, down for leaving, square-root scale, full at 8 levels) and a signed number.
  - The line below reads "Tax N% · Net $X".
  - A tooltip explains the meter.
- **Budget [B].** A tax row with − Lower, + Raise and Standard 10% buttons (disabled at their limits). The rate note gives the per-resident and per-job amounts and the demand percentage. The forecast and last settlement each show the rate used.
- **Status, inspector and Help.** Rate changes report the new rate, amounts and demand effect. The inspector adds "leaving" and "surplus" explanations. Help has a tax line.
- **Load messages.** A format-6 load now says "every lot kept, tax rate set to the standard 10%" instead of a zero cleared-tile count.

**Saves.**

- **Schema 7 records.** Schema 7 requires `tax_rate`, and every non-empty ledger records its `tax_rate`. Both use strict ranges.
- **Migration from schema 6.** Schema 6 loads unchanged, plus rate 10 and a ledger rate of 10.
- **Migration from schemas 1-5.** They keep the D30 clearing migration and gain the same fields.
- **Rejection.** Earlier formats containing any tax field are rejected.
- **Preservation.** Older primary and backup bytes are preserved as `manual.schemaN.json`, now including schema 6.
- **Fixtures.** The seven earlier fixtures are byte-identical, and `m2f-schema7.json` is added (see the fixture README).

## Native EXE evidence

- **How it was driven.** The actual `builds/windows/citybuilder.exe` was started from an ordinary shell and driven by posted window messages to its own window, with `PrintWindow` captures. The real cursor was never moved.
- **Profile.** The process resolved `%APPDATA%\OriginalCityBuilder\`. That profile had no `saves` folder before the run. The town used was a schema-6 town (48 residents / 18 jobs, day 91) from the artwork evaluation.
- **Load.** Posted Ctrl+L did not load, because Godot reads modifier keys from the real keyboard state, so the Load button was used.
- **Captures.** Ignored, under `builds/validation/m2f/native/`.

Run on the first M2f export (with linear meter bars and the earlier load wording):

1. A fresh launch showed H +3 · S 0 · W 0 and "Tax 10% · Net $100.00".
2. Loading the schema-6 town kept every lot and showed the standard rate.
3. Budget → + Raise five times set 15%. The meter read H −2 · S −1 · W −1, and the forecast was $352.00 income ($3 / $6 per person). The last settlement still showed 10%.
4. About seven seconds at 3x took the town to day 113 with 28 residents / 12 jobs, and the meter read 0 · 0 · 0. Save wrote schema 7 / `m2f-1` with `tax_rate` 15 and a ledger at 10%. `manual.schema6.json` was byte-identical to the loaded town (SHA256 `d2a8a5b8…`).
5. After a restart and Load, the town returned at 15%, with an unchanged Budget and ledger.

Final smoke run on the packaged export:

6. The format-6 load message read "every lot kept, tax rate set to the standard 10%".
7. At 15% the down bars were clearly visible.
8. Budget at 0% showed H +5 · S +1 · W +1 and "Tax 0% · Net −$133.00 · DEFICIT".

The process was then ended. The profile was restored: no `saves` folder, the leftover `.recovery_mode_lock` removed, and this session's packed-test folders moved to `builds/validation/m2f/profile-tests/`.

## Limits

- **Native coverage.** Native evidence is 1280x720 at standard text on one machine; larger text is covered by automated layout checks only.
- **Tuning.** The tax numbers are provisional and untuned by human play. The equilibrium at standard tax is still the small M2c town, so larger towns need lower rates, more land and money.
- **Unverified.** Human usability and enjoyment, DPI variants, other hardware and operating systems, the controller, performance and CI.
- **Not covered by this work.** The five-person M3 playtest gate.
