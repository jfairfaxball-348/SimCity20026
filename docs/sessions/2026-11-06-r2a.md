## Decision before code

[R2A_SPEC.md](../R2A_SPEC.md) and D36 were written before model code:

- The D33 field becomes land value.
- Commercial lots add customers: +1 per developed residential lot within 3 side-steps, up to +2. They are ordered by value, held below 0 at level 1, and drop like residential lots; the drops count towards D32 leaving.
- Industry ignores land value and is ordered by freight: road steps from the link road to the nearest connected road beside the lot.
- Schema 9 / `r2a-1`, with no new fields.

**Roadmap deviation, recorded in the roadmap.** The roadmap suggested a pull towards the town centre. R2a uses commercial trade and industrial freight instead, because both are explainable per lot and cheap.

**Wording change during implementation.** The customers part is shown to players as "trade". The longest legend line ("Land value −1: park +2, water +1, customers +2, industry −6") measured 513 px against a 488 px label at the largest text, and wrapped. With "trade" it measures 469 px.
