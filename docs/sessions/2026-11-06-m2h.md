## Implemented behaviour

**Summary.** `game/scripts/presentation/town_summary.gd` builds a pure, read-only list from existing derived state.

- **Blockers.**
  - no road on the gold flag, replacing the access item;
  - lots without road access;
  - lots without power;
  - lots short of power;
  - home lots held or dropping by workshops (D33);
  - levels leaving under taxes above 10% (D32);
  - per zone type, demand with no serviced lot able to grow.
- **Counts.** Each item has a count, the number of affected lots that already hold buildings, an example (the lowest anchor, or the flag) and a remedy.
- **Ranking.** Items are ranked by levels affected: 2 per lot for access and power items, 1 per held home, and the surplus or demand levels for the rest. Table order breaks ties, at most five are kept, and a deficit item follows them.
- **Status items.** An empty town gets the starting steps. A town with no blockers and no deficit is told nothing is holding it back.

**Interface.**

- **Opening it.** **T**, or the **Town summary [T]** button at the right of the status bar, opens a modal *Town summary* dialog.
- **Contents.** A heading gives the day, residents, jobs and tax rate, then one row per item.
- **Actions.** **Show** closes the dialog, centres the view on the example and inspects it. **Budget** opens Budget.
- **Other.** Time is suspended while the dialog is open. Help gains one line.

No simulation rule, save field, schema or fixture changed.

## Limits

- **Native coverage.** Native evidence is 1280x720 at standard text on one machine.
- **Wording and ordering.** These are untested with players. The no-room item always shows the flag rather than suggesting free land.
- **Not covered.** Business desirability, civic services and traffic are not part of the summary because they do not exist yet.
- **Unverified.** Human usability and enjoyment, DPI variants, other hardware and operating systems, the controller, performance and CI.
- **Not covered by this work.** The five-person M3 playtest gate.
