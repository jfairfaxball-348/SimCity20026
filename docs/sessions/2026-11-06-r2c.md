## Decision before code

[R2C_SPEC.md](../R2C_SPEC.md) and D38 were written and adopted before model code:

- police station (ID 7) and fire station (ID 8): 3x3, $400, $30 a month each, keys 9 and 0;
- a station works with road access and one power unit, allocated with zone lots in the existing nearest-first order;
- a working station covers zone lots within its reach, footprint to footprint, ignoring terrain; no stacking;
- level 4 for every zone type also needs police and fire cover; lots above the new cap drop one level per round, counted with D32 leaving;
- Services overlay (C), inspector reasons, summary items, Budget lines, Help and original art;
- schema 11 / `r2c-1` with the new IDs and ledger counts `police` and `fire`.

**Revisions during implementation, before delivery.**

- **Reach 12, not 10.** With 10, no single central police and fire pair could cover all six lots of the standard 18-tile street from a side road; 12 covers it with the stations side by side. Recorded in the specification and D38.
- **Sidebar.** Two extra tool rows pushed the sidebar past the footer at 720p (packed checks). Park, Police and Fire now share one compact row ("8 Park", "9 Police", "0 Fire"), with prices in tooltips and the placement preview. The measured sidebar height is identical to 0.14.0-r2b.
- **Inspector.** A separate "Cover" line overflowed the inspector at the largest text, and an inline suffix still wrapped at text 20. The lot legend shows cover only while the Services overlay is on (as V does for land value); the gate reason ("L4 needs police and fire cover (missing fire).") always names what is missing.
- The shortened idle text is "Not working: needs road access and power. Upkeep $30/month."
