## Implemented behaviour

- **Evaluation.** `game/scripts/presentation/opening_guide.gd` is a pure evaluation of the first unmet step:
  1. a flag road;
  2. a home lot with road access;
  3. that home powered;
  4. any home developed;
  5. the project dialog opened this session, or the project completed.
  - It also gives a no-link state for an all-water boundary.
  - Targets are the flag, or the first qualifying home lot anchor (bracket size 3).
- **The card.** `main.gd` builds a non-modal card in `ui_panels`, so clicks over it never paint.
  - **Placement.** x = sidebar right + 12, bottom 12 px above the status bar, width min(480, space left of the inspector).
  - **Contents.** "FIRST STEPS · N OF 5 / DONE", the step text at the interface size, Show or Open Project, and Hide or Close.
  - **Rechecking.** Steps are rechecked after every refresh of the HUD (commands, clock, tax and project changes), after load, and after the project dialog opens.
  - **Session-only.** Visibility and the project flag last for the session.
- **Re-entry.** **F1** (`toggle_guide`) toggles the card and is ignored while a dialog is open. Help has a **Show first steps** action beside OK.
- **Show.** Show centres and inspects the target (shared `_show_cell`). The world draws a static two-tone corner-bracket marker on the target while the card is visible.
- **Untouched code.** No file under `sim/`, `persistence/` or `content/` changed (`git diff --stat` empty for those paths).

## Native EXE evidence

- **How it was driven.** The packaged `builds/windows/citybuilder.exe` was started from an ordinary shell at 1280x720 and driven by posted window messages to its own window, with `PrintWindow` captures. F1 was added to the driver.
- **Profile.** The process resolved `%APPDATA%\OriginalCityBuilder\`, which had no `saves` folder before the run.
- **Captures.** Ignored, under `builds/validation/m3b/native/`.

1. A fresh launch showed "FIRST STEPS · 1 OF 5" at the bottom left, with corner brackets on the flag. Show recentred on the flag.
2. A flag road by pointer gave step 2. A home lot gave step 3, and its Show recentred on the lot. Near the map's right edge the camera is clamped, so the lot's lower tiles sat under the inspector, while the anchor tile stayed visible. This is the spec's recorded limit.
3. **A walkthrough slip.** The scripted station click assumed the pre-Show camera and landed off the street, so the card correctly stayed at step 3. A J press in the same script also set the session's project flag. After recentring, clearing the stray station and placing one beside the street, the card showed step 4 ("Run time (Space or 1x)… H on the meter is demand").
4. Space for about 6 seconds grew the home to level 1, and the card showed "FIRST STEPS · DONE" with only Close.
5. Close hid the card. F1 showed it and F1 hid it. Help → Show first steps closed Help and showed it.
6. Save wrote schema 8 / `m2g-1` with no guide field.
7. **Restart and load.** Closing and restarting showed step 1 on the fresh town. Load rechecked the steps to step 5, because the project flag resets each session. J then gave Done.
8. **Largest text.** A fresh launch at the largest text showed the step-1 card fitting beside the wider inspector. That run revealed the 2 px gap defect above, now fixed and automatically checked.

After the final export, a largest-text recheck showed the fixed card placement. The process was ended each time without further saving. The profile was restored: the walkthrough save moved to `builds/validation/m3b/native/saved-walkthrough.json`, no `saves` folder, no leftover lock, and this session's packed-test folders moved to `builds/validation/m3b/profile-tests/`.

## Limits

- **Native coverage.** Native evidence is 1280x720 on one machine.
- **Show near map edges.** Show centring is clamped at map edges, as recorded in the spec.
- **Remembered dismissal.** Not remembered across launches; this belongs with future settings and autosaves.
- **Unverified.** Human usability and enjoyment (including whether the start-up card feels intrusive), DPI variants, other hardware and operating systems, the controller, performance and CI.
- **Not covered by this work.** The five-person M3 playtest gate.
