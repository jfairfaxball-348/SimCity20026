## Implemented behaviour

**Footprints.**

- Homes, shops and workshops are 3x3 lots anchored at their top-left tile; power stations are 4x4. Parks and roads stay one tile.
- Every tile of a footprint carries its occupancy ID. Development and service-loss state live only on lot anchors.
- Derived anchor lists and a tile-to-anchor map are rebuilt with the other derived state and never saved.

**Commands.**

- Zone and station commands take anchors. Clear takes tiles and expands to every footprint it touches.
- Each footprint is validated whole (land, empty, inside the map, not covering the outside-link flag), and lots in one stroke may not overlap.
- Identical repaints are free, and a mixed stroke charges only new lots.
- A lot costs $25 and a station $500, charged per footprint.

**Services.**

- A lot has access or power through any side-sharing perimeter tile; corners do not count.
- All 16 station tiles are network tiles at distance 0. Capacity and upkeep count stations by anchor, not tiles.

**Growth, budget, park and project.** These run per lot, with all D19-D28 numbers unchanged. A lot is covered by a park, or counts for the project, when any of its tiles is within distance 3.

**Presentation and wording.**

- Original code-drawn buildings: an outlined empty parcel with its zone symbol, plus pitched-roof homes, awning shops and sawtooth workshops at two levels. The 4x4 station is a two-chimney hall with a bolt sign.
- Marks are drawn once per lot. Hovering any tile inspects the lot or station.
- Zone tools centre the lot on the pointer, and dragging lays a straight run of lots along the dominant axis. The preview shows every footprint tile and the price, for example "4 lots: $100.00".
- Inspector, Help, status, project and README wording now says "lot".
- **Deviation:** the sidebar keeps its "$25" labels, because "$25/lot" would crowd the largest-text sidebar. The per-lot price appears in tooltips, Help, previews and the README, and the specification records this.

**Saves.**

- Schema 6 adds a strict `footprint_anchor` array. Validation rejects missing, wrong-sized or out-of-range arrays. It also rejects orphan zone or station tiles, misaligned or overlapping anchors, footprints off the map, mixed IDs, and development on non-anchor tiles.
- Schemas 1-5 validate under their own meanings, then clear single-tile zones and stations. Roads, parks, cash, time, RNG, sequence, ledger and project record are kept.
- The load message reports the number of cleared tiles.
- The generic store now preserves schema-5 primary/backup bytes as `manual.schema5.json`; no store change was needed.
- **Validation change:** for migrated saves, the old "serviced plot cannot retain a loss counter" check no longer runs, because those zones are cleared.
- **Fixtures:** all six historical fixtures stay byte-identical. A new `m2e-schema6.json` was captured by the M2e model; its next tick settles exactly 4400 cents.

## Limits and stop point

M2e's bounded local implementation is complete. Not claimed:

- largest-text native input, native hover inspection, a native project completion, Ctrl+S/Ctrl+L native shortcuts or physical-input testing;
- DPI variants, other hardware or operating systems, the controller route or performance targets;
- enjoyment, or the five-person gate.

Keeping $25 and one power unit per lot keeps each building's economics unchanged but needs more land and frontage per building; observe this in playtests rather than tuning it here. No public binary release.

The next session implements the specified M3b guide by default; the owner may instead choose a genre candidate to scope first.
