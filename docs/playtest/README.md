# Solo playtest kit (roadmap R6)

Prepared 7 November 2026 under [D42](../DECISIONS.md#d42). Under owner decision **U39** the R6 / M3 playtest is the owner playing alone, in place of the five-person observed test in the [product brief](../PRODUCT_BRIEF.md#playtest-gates).

## Contents

- [`PLAYTEST.txt`](PLAYTEST.txt): the owner's guide, four sessions with goals, what to notice, a notes sheet adapted from the product brief's gates for one player, where saves are, how to hand back, and known limits. It is copied into every Windows export as `PLAYTEST.txt` beside the game.

## How the results will be recorded

When the owner reports back (for example "playtest done" with notes in a chat), a later session will:

1. Record the notes in an evidence record under `docs/sessions/`, quoting the owner's own words where useful and keeping their meaning.
2. Add a Decisions entry for the R6 result: which brief questions were met, partly met or not met, for **one player**. One person's view is not wider evidence, and the owner knows the project's goals, so first-time clarity in particular may be overstated or understated.
3. Turn the main problems into a short, ordered fix list, and agree with the owner whether to fix them before R7 (as the delivery plan advises if the result is weak).
4. Look at any saves, autosaves or settings the owner copies into the project (for example `builds/playtest/`, which is ignored by Git), without publishing them.

No automated run, test or agent play counts as this playtest.
