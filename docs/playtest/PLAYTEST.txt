YOUR SOLO PLAYTEST - UNTITLED CITY BUILDER 0.19.0-r6

Thank you for playing. This is the M3 playtest (roadmap R6). You are the
only tester (owner decision U39), so your honest impressions are the result.
There are no right answers. Being bored, confused or annoyed is just as
useful to write down as enjoying it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BEFORE YOU START
------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Unzip the build anywhere and run citybuilder.exe. Keep citybuilder.pck
  beside it. README.txt in the same folder lists every control.
- Windows may warn that the program is unsigned. That is expected for this
  local build.
- Try not to read README.txt or Help before Session 1. The point is to see
  how far the game itself guides you.
- Keep this file open beside the game, or print the notes sheet (section 4).
- Sound starts at Master 80%, Sounds 70%, Music 30%. M mutes everything.
  Display > Sound & motion changes the volumes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FOUR SESSIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Play them on different days if you can. Each one has a goal; it is fine
not to reach it. Stop whenever you like, and write a few notes afterwards.

Session 1 - A first town (about 15 minutes, no Help)
  Start the game and follow only the First steps card at the bottom left.
  Goal: residential lots growing beside a road with power.
  Notice: the first moment you did not know what to do next.

Session 2 - Growing up (20 to 30 minutes)
  Goal: get a lot to level 4 (two yellow pips). That needs land value (parks
  or water nearby), and police and fire stations (keys 9 and 0) within reach.
  Also complete the optional neighbourhood project (J).
  Use the Town summary (T) and the inspector when something will not grow.
  Notice: whether you could find out why a lot was stuck, and fix it.

Session 3 - A longer town (30 minutes or more)
  Goal: keep a town going for ten game years (to January 1960) at 3x.
  Try the tax rate in Budget (B), watch the R / C / I meter, and look at the
  Graphs (H) now and then. Autosaves happen every 60 game days.
  Notice: when it became interesting, when it became dull, and whether you
  wanted to keep going.

Session 4 - Your choice (optional, about 20 minutes)
  If you have a gamepad, play with only the gamepad (Back opens the Menu,
  which reaches everything). Otherwise, try Display > Controls and change a
  few keys, or play however you like.
  Notice: anything awkward, missing or surprising.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT TO NOTICE (any session)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Moments you were stuck, and what got you unstuck.
- Anything that felt unfair, slow, fiddly or confusing.
- Anything you liked, even small things.
- How the sounds, music and animation felt: pleasant, annoying, too loud,
  too quiet, too repetitive?
- Anything that looked broken, wrong or out of place.
- Whether you lost any work (saves, autosaves) or got stuck with no money.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHERE YOUR FILES ARE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Help > Open save folder (or Back > Open save folder on a gamepad) opens it.
It is usually:

  %APPDATA%\OriginalCityBuilder\saves\
      manual.json          your manual save (Save button / Ctrl+S)
      manual.json.bak      the previous manual save
      autosave1-3.json     the three rotating autosaves
  %APPDATA%\OriginalCityBuilder\settings.json   your remembered settings

------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HANDING BACK
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. When you are done, start a chat
with Claude in this project and say something like "playtest done", then
paste your notes. If a save shows a problem, mention it and where it is;
Claude can read files in the project folder if you copy them there (for
example into builds/playtest/, which is not committed).

Your results will be written up honestly in the project's Decisions and an
evidence record, as one player's view, and will decide what to fix before
the next roadmap step (R7).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. KNOWN LIMITS OF THIS BUILD
------------------------------------------------------------------------

- No real gamepad has been tried yet; only simulated input was tested.
- Nobody has listened to the sounds and music yet. You are the first.
- Tested only at 1280x720 on one Windows PC.
- If you change keys, Help lists your keys at the top but its other text and
  README.txt still name the defaults; the First steps card and the buttons
  show your current keys.
- Disasters, traffic, pollution and crime are not in the game yet.
